Step-by-Step Formula Breakdown

Copy these exact formulas into your trading logbook to understand how your position size is calculated.

STEP 1
Maximum Dollar Risk
Calculate total cash capital exposed to loss on this single trade setup.
Dollar Risk = Capital × (Risk % ÷ 100)
STEP 2
Price Distance Risk
Find the dollar difference between your Entry Price and invalidation Stop Loss.
Distance = | Entry Price - Stop Loss Price |
STEP 3
TradingView Order Quantity
Divide total risk amount by price distance to derive exact contract/unit quantity.
Order Qty = Max Dollar Risk ÷ Price Distance
